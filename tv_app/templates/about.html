About iBOX TV

A faster path from “what should I watch?” to what is available.

iBOX TV organizes availability updates into a searchable catalogue enriched with useful title details.

01

Discover

Browse crawlable TV, anime, movie, and genre collections focused on currently available listings.

03

Continue

Open the matching Telegram flow and follow the channel for future availability updates.

Metadata and content concerns

Title metadata and artwork are supplied using The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Official trailer embeds are hosted by YouTube. Rights holders and users can report an incorrect listing or content concern through the official Telegram channel.
